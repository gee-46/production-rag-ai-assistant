# Medical Device Technical Specification: MED-PULSE-88

## 1. Electrical & Operating Characteristics
- **Device Model Identifier**: `MED-PULSE-88`
- **Battery System**: 3.7V Lithium-ion rechargeable cell with over-current protection.
- **Operating Temperature Range**: `-10°C to 50°C` (ambient humidity 15% to 90% non-condensing).
- **Sampling Frequency**: Real-time physiological telemetry samples at `250 Hz`.

## 2. Regulatory Compliance & Sterilisation
- **Sterilisation Protocol**: Validated under standard `ISO-11135` Ethylene Oxide (EtO) process.
- **Quality Management System**: Compliant with `ISO-13485:2016` medical device standard.
- **Alarm Classification**: High-priority acoustic alarms emit a 5-pulse tone burst repeating every `2.5 seconds`.
